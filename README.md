
# "That Guy": The Catalytic Node in Group Ecology

*A conceptual exploration of emotional contagion, social ecology, norm diffusion, and group climate.*

---

## Overview

Why can a single individual alter the climate of an entire group?

This project explores the concept of the **Catalytic Node**, an actor whose behavior raises the emotional temperature of a social environment and creates conditions in which negativity, conflict, exclusion, or harmful dynamics become more likely to spread.

Rather than focusing on personality or pathology, the framework examines how particular individuals function within a broader social ecology.

The central proposition is simple:

> Some individuals do not merely participate in a social environment. They alter it.

This repository develops a conceptual model for understanding how one actor may influence group norms, emotional climate, communication patterns, and institutional responses.

---

## Core Concept

### The Catalytic Node

A **Catalytic Node** is an individual whose presence significantly influences the social climate of a group.

The catalytic node may:

- Raise emotional temperature
- Shift behavioral norms
- Trigger defensive adaptations
- Amplify conflict
- Accelerate negativity spread
- Alter participation patterns

The concept is ecological rather than individualistic.

The framework asks:

> What conditions allow a catalyst to influence the group?

rather than:

> What is wrong with the individual?
